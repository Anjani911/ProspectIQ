# Brag Plan: ProspectIQ

## What is this app?
ProspectIQ is an AI-powered local business intelligence and discovery platform that crawls local business prospects, audits their websites across SEO and security, calculates a health score (0–100), and automatically generates prioritized, pitch-ready sales opportunities.

## The angle
Agencies and freelancers pitch blind. Cold outreach with generic emails gets ignored. ProspectIQ turns cold outreach into pre-audited client opportunities: with one search, you discover local businesses, instantly audit their web flaws (missing HTTPS, broken SEO, missing alt tags, thin content), and get ready-made high-priority pitch opportunities before your competitors even know they exist.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A bold, clean editorial split: "Stop pitching blind." followed by "Cold outreach is dead. Intelligence closes deals." Delivered with crisp Bricolage Grotesque typography on the signature ProspectIQ warm cream canvas.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: Instant Discovery** — Simulated search for "Boutique" in "Chicago, IL", immediately pulling live business prospects with contact info and online presence.
- **Beat 2: Deep Website Intelligence Audit** — One-click website audit runs diagnostics and lands an alarming `42/100` health score ("Outdated").
- **Beat 3: Auto-Generated Sales Opportunities** — The system immediately extracts high-intent client pitches: "Website does not use HTTPS" (High Priority), "Images missing alt text" (Medium Priority), and "Missing meta description".

## Outro / punchline
"ProspectIQ. Discover leads. Audit flaws. Close deals." The terracotta brand spark hits with confident permanence and the live workspace indicator connects.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Prospect discovery search (`Category: Boutique` | `Location: Chicago, IL`).
2. **Key action**: Trigger "Analyze Website" → progress scan generates real-time diagnostics and health score (`42/100`).
3. **Result**: Auto-populated Opportunity Pipeline featuring prioritized client pitch cards with status tracking.
